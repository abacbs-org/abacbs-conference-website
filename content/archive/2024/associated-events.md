## Symposium on Bioinformatics Excellence and Innovation (SBEI24)

Monday 4 November 2024, Sydney.

#SBEI24 is a full day "Symposium on Bioinformatics Excellence and Innovation" that was organised alongside the annual ABACBS Conference 2024.

The symposium focused on applications of bioinformatics in industry. Broadly the day included a mix of keynotes and panel discussions covering:

- Cloud Bioinformatics
- Sustainable Bioinformatics
- Bioinformatics as a Service
- Bioinformatics Futures

### Target Audience

- Bioinformatics core facilities
- Data science teams working in bioinformatics and computational biology
- Biotech industry
- AgriBio industry
- Bioinformaticians and computational biologists in academia
- Biomedical data managers
- Clinical facilities
- Software engineers with an interest in genetics/genomics

## EMCR Hackathon

The ABACBS Post-doc Subcommittee ran a hackathon on 4 November at the University of Sydney, supported by the Theo Murphy Initiative (Australia), administered by the Australian Academy of Science. It ran as a satellite meeting the day before the ABACBS 2024 conference, concurrently with COMBINE and SBEI.

The hackathon featured multiple challenges spanning data visualization, programming, and omics analyses. Thanks to support from the Theo Murphy Initiative, several registration fee waiver scholarships were offered to attend the conference. All postdocs, of all career stages and programming levels (beginner to advanced), were welcome to join for a day of collaboration, networking, and innovation.

To be eligible for a scholarship, postdocs needed to: attend the EMCR Hackathon and the 2024 ABACBS Conference; submit poster abstracts to the ABACBS Conference; present the poster at the EMCR Hackathon; and be a post-doctoral researcher (of any career stage). Scholarship awardees were provided with a promo code to have their registration fee waived. A registration fee of $20 AUD to the EMCR Hackathon applied.
