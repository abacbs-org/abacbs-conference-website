## Abstract Submission Dates

Talk, poster and workshop abstract submission deadline: Midnight 19 August 2024 (extended to 26 August 2024)

Talk and poster presenters notified of outcomes by 30 September 2024.

## Conference Registration Dates

Registration opens: 26 August 2024

Early bird deadline: 8 October 2024

Conference dates: 4-6 November 2024, Abercrombie Building, University of Sydney, Sydney NSW.

## Carers Support - Expression of Interest

ABACBS acknowledges carer responsibilities have the potential to hinder academic and professional bioinformaticians from being able to take full advantage of the opportunities presented at scientific conferences and networking events.

We seek expressions of interest and advice on how the Society can best provide carers support at ABACBS 2024. We invite all interested parties to complete this short [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfC_50sztfDh0aqQiGKl3lVssF9rUjdKH0QCzJEcKyfmvSbkg/viewform).
