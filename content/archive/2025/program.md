## Venue

ABCABS 2025 will be held at the Adelaide Oval Conference Centre (Mon–Wed). Enter via the South Gate (marked below with a blue X). This is where registration and signage will be. From the CBD, the simplest route is through the train station and across the Riverbank pedestrian bridge (~600 m, and quite a scenic walk). The ABACBS 2025 workshops and BioC Asia (Thurs-Fri) will be held at Flinders University Festival Plaza (labelled below with "workshops")
