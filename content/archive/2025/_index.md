Welcome to ABACBS 2025

 ABACBS 2025 was held in Adelaide at the iconic Adelaide Oval during the week of November 24, 2025. 

The 2025 ABACBS conference took place on Kaurna country. We pay respects to the Kaurna people of the Adelaide plains and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. 

The program  followed our traditional format:

Monday, Nov 24 – COMBINE, the EMCR Shark Tank and Symposium on Bioinformatics and Engineered Intelligence (SBEI) followed by ABACBS Welcome Reception. (Adelaide Oval)

Tuesday & Wednesday, Nov 25–26 – Main ABACBS conference with multiple parallel sessions. (Adelaide Oval)

Thursday & Friday, Nov 27–28 – Hands-on workshops to be held at Flinders University City Plaza in the Adelaide CBD.


ABACBS is also delighted to support and partner with The Australasian Human Microbiome Research Network (AHMRN). The AHMRN 2025 symposium will be held on Friday 28th of November 9am to 12:30pm in the Adelaide CBD. We have scheduled our microbiome focused workshops/events around this enabling participants to attend both. For more details [click here](https://bit.ly/ahmrn2025)
