title: "ABACBS Society Awards 2025"
---

One of our most important roles as a society is to celebrate the achievements of our members and help build strong, competitive track records—especially for early- and mid-career researchers. Since 2017, we’ve run annual awards to do just that, and each year we call for nominations from across our community.

Whether you’re planning a fellowship application, considering a move to industry, or have always wanted to deliver your best Oscar-acceptance speech—this is for you.

[**You can find more information on past award winners here**](https://aba-cbs.squarespace.com/awards)

## 2025 Award Categories

- [**Outstanding Contribution by an Early- or Mid-Career Researcher**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xV4uQGenv-p1ByI-1eH5rXEBWjLw8iB4/view?usp=share_link)

  *Up to three awards will be given. Applicants will be split by career stage and ranked relative to opportunity, depending on application volume.*

- [**Outstanding PhD Thesis Award**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MdtSSWNRrBGldLIUVg3vMyeWCkSlNCs2/view?usp=share_link)

- [**Outstanding Professional Bioinformatician Award**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wksacSAH3EyAI3BQ-czfGbkzo_XL4jhD/view?usp=share_link)

- [**Outstanding Bioinformatics Software Maintainer Award**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UNgkIrnPRQDu3IG-ngy4k0sahvHl7MtU/view?usp=share_link)

- [**The “Torsten Seemann” Outstanding Bioinformatics Software Developer Award**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11dFDoHslaRTcVubn5AvIq4QM2f6feR86/view?usp=share_link)

- [**The “Nicholas Wong” Outstanding Contributions to the Bioinformatics Community Award**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AQCxGK4je9r9MqbqiIj26jDN25_w3L3/view?usp=share_link)

  *This new award honours Nick Wong, an important member of our community, and recognises those who support and grow our community, just as Nick did.*

## Submission process

Please click on the award above to be linked to a document outlining eligibility and the submission details.

Submissions are due on August 29th. One extension only! now due on ***September 12th***

The winners will be announced at the conference dinner on ***November 25th.***

Award judges will be drawn from a combination of past award winners, elected members of the ABACBS Executive Committee and members of our wider community.
