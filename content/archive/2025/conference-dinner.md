---
title: "ABACBS 2025 Conference Dinner"
---

We are delighted to welcome ABACBS 2025 attendees to our official Conference Dinner on Tuesday, 25th November at the iconic Cloisters Courtyard on the University of Adelaide campus. Enjoy a relaxed evening featuring a deluxe South Australian-inspired BBQ and a selection of the region’s finest wines.

This special night is a chance to connect, network, and celebrate a decade of achievement in Australian bioinformatics and computational biology. The evening will also include the presentation of the [2025 ABACBS Awards]({{< relref "awards" >}}), recognising excellence across our community.

Dinner tickets ($95) can be added when you register through the [main conference registration portal](https://iscb.swoogo.com/abacbs2025).

![The Cloisters Courtyard at the University of Adelaide](../cloisters.webp)

*The Cloisters Courtyard at the University of Adelaide*

The dinner venue is a short 13-minute walk from the main conference site, along the beautiful River Torrens through parklands.

We’ll be arranging guided groups to walk from the conference venue to the dinner. If you prefer to make your own way, use the map below or [open walking directions from Adelaide Oval](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Adelaide+Oval,+Adelaide,+Australia&destination=-34.918351,138.6035414&travelmode=walking).
