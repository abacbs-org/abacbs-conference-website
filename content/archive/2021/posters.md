As a hybrid conference, posters were presented digitally via [Gather.Town](https://www.gather.town/), alongside in-person viewing at each hub site.

## Poster upload

Poster slides were uploaded to the Gather.Town platform as PNG images (Gather.Town only supported PNG format), a minimum of 600×1000px and a maximum of 3MB in size, named in the format `<Surname>_<First Name>_poster_<a random number between 1 and 10>_<a random letter>.PNG`. Uploading a file with the same name again replaced the existing poster. Posters were due before 5pm Friday 19 November.

## Short/lightning talk slides

Presenters of short or lightning talks sent their slides directly to their session chairs by 5pm Friday 19 November.

## Poster sessions

Poster sessions ran alongside the conference program on Gather.Town during breaks on 23 and 24 November — see [Program](/archive/2021/program/) for session times.
