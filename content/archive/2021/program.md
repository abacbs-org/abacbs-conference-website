All times AEDT unless noted. Each session was chaired remotely on Zoom from a different hub location around the country; invited speakers and abstract talks presented via Zoom, or at a local hub site if available (Adelaide, Brisbane, Perth and Hobart). Attendees interacted via Gather.Town and viewed posters in each ABACBS/COMBINE/Phylomania room.
