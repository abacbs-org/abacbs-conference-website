---
title: "Abstract Submission"
---

Abstracts were welcomed broadly related to bioinformatics, computational biology and phylogenetics, across fields including (but not restricted to) big data, biomedical, clinical informatics, COVID-19, data integration, epidemiology, evolution, genomics, image analysis, metabolomics, methods development, microbial genomics or metagenomics, network biology, non-model organisms, phylogenetics, proteomics, single-cell, statistical bioinformatics, transcriptomics, and workflow design.

Abstracts were submitted via the dedicated [ABACBS 2021 abstract submission system](https://conference2021.abacbs.org/abstract), which required a registered ORCID ID to log in. Abstract submission closed ahead of the conference; presenters of accepted talks and posters were notified by email.
