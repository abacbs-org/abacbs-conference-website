---
title: "Code of Conduct"
---

The ABACBS conference aims to promote rich, critical, and positive intellectual discourse. Harassment and other forms of aggressive or inappropriate behaviour among conference attendees will not be tolerated. Conference attendees must agree to the ABACBS Code of Conduct as part of conference registration.

Please feel free to engage the ABACBS safety officers listed below if you have any questions regarding the code of conduct or to report any possible Code of Conduct violation.

**Current Safety Officers:**
