title: "Registration"
---

The organizing committee worked to keep the registration fee for the joint conference as low as possible, depending only on ABACBS membership status. All fees are shown in Australian dollars.

## Conference registration fees

| Registration type | Fee |
|---|---|
| ABACBS Members (Full or Student) | $20 |
| Non-member student | $70 |
| Non-member | $130 |
| Phylomania conference and COMBINE/AYRCOB Student Symposium | Free |

Associate members of ABACBS were subject to the non-member registration fee.

## Workshop registration fees

| Registration type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Workshop (members) | $10 per workshop per person |
| Workshop (non-members) | $15 per workshop per person |

Participants could register just for the workshop(s) without registering for the conference.

## ORCID requirement

Registrants were required to input their ORCID ID when registering, used to enable personalised login to digital content during the hybrid conference, and to submit an abstract.

## Online streaming

The conference was live-streamed via Zoom for registered participants, with in-person hub sites in Adelaide, Brisbane, Perth and Hobart. Zoom and Gather.Town links (for video conferencing and poster/social interaction respectively) were made available to registered participants who authenticated with their ORCID.

Registrations closed ahead of the conference.
