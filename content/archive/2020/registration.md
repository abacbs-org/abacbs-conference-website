## Conference registration

|                                   | Cost |
| --------------------------------- | ---- |
| ABACBS Members (Full or Student)  | $20  |
| Non-member student                | $70  |
| Non-member                        | $130 |
| COMBINE Students’ Symposium       | FREE |

Associate members of ABACBS will be subject to the non-member registration fee. Please visit the member registration system for new member signup, upgrades from Associate to Full member, and membership renewal.

We will send you an e-mail with your login details to the conference talk video website two weeks prior to the live sessions. Please ensure your e-mail address is entered correctly in the registration form.

Registrations for the conference will close on the 26 November 9 am (AEDT) Sydney local time.
