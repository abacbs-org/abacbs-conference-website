The ABACBS Annual Conference will be held virtually this year. We aim to provide attendees with opportunities to interact virtually and with a platform to share cutting-edge bioinformatics and computational biology research, with an emphasis on how computational research is done. This year, the Australian National University will be the virtual host.Welcome to ABACBS 2015, held 10-11 October 2015 at the Garvan Institute of Medical Research, Sydney, NSW, preceding AGTA 2015.

The conference was convened by Nicola Armstrong and Richard Edwards.
