ABACBS 2023 ran alongside the following concurrent events, all in Brisbane:

## COMBINE Student Symposium

A one-day Student Symposium run by the Australian Computational Biology and Bioinformatics Student Society (COMBINE), held 4 December at Hilton Brisbane. See the [COMBINE website](https://www.combine.org.au/) for more information.

## Symposium on Bioinformatics Engineering in Industry (SBEI23)

Monday 4 December 2023, Hilton Brisbane, held in parallel to COMBINE.

SBEI23 was a full day "Symposium on Bioinformatics Engineering in Industry" organised alongside the annual ABACBS Conference 2023, focused on applications of bioinformatics in industry — a mix of keynotes and panel discussions covering:

- Bioinformatics in Tx and Dx Development
- Informatics Platforms: Standardisation at Scale
- Artificial Intelligence Applications in Biotechnology
- Professional Bioinformatics in Action & Industry Strategy Discussions

### Target Audience

- Bioinformatics core facilities
- Data science teams working in bioinformatics and computational biology
- Biotech industry
- AgriBio industry
- Bioinformaticians and computational biologists in academia
- Biomedical data managers
- Clinical facilities
- Software engineers with an interest in genetics/genomics
