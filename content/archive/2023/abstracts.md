---
title: "Abstract Submission"
---

Abstracts were welcomed from any disciplines tightly linked to bioinformatics and computational biology, including (but not restricted to):

- Algorithmic design
- Artificial Intelligence
- Big data analysis
- Biomedical informatics
- Clinical informatics
- COVID19
- Data integration and/or curation
- Epidemiology
- Evolution
- Genomics
- Image analysis
- Integrated omics
- Long-read sequencing
- Machine learning
- Metabolomics
- Microbial genomics
- Metagenomics
- Network biology
- Non-model organisms
- Phylogenetics/phylogenomics
- Proteomics
- Single-cell omics
- Spatial transcriptomics
- Statistical bioinformatics
- Transcriptomics
- Workflow Design

Abstracts could be submitted for consideration as oral or poster presentations, and were eligible for various awards. Abstracts were also allocated to one of the ABACBS conference themes: Machine Learning for Discovery; Epigenomics & Long Read Sequencing; Phylogenetics & Evolution; Agricultural and Environmental Omics; Human Health and Translation; Cutting Edge Omics Technologies and Analysis; Big Data Infrastructure.

Abstract submissions closed prior to the conference.

Queries: conference@abacbs.org
