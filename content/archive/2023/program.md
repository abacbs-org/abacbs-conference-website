The ABACBS 2023 conference ran 4-8 December 2023 in Brisbane. COMBINE, SBEI and the main ABACBS sessions were held at Hilton Brisbane; workshops ran 7-8 December at the University of Queensland St Lucia campus — see [Workshops](/archive/2023/workshops/) for that schedule.

## Monday 4 December

- **08:30–17:00** — COMBINE Student Symposium (Ballrooms A & B)
- **08:30–17:00** — Symposium on Bioinformatics Engineering and Industry (SBEI) (Ballroom C)
- **17:00–19:00** — Welcome Reception (Level 5 Foyer)
