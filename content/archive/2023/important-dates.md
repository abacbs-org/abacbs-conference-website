## Conference Dates

- COMBINE Student Symposium — 4 December 2023
- Symposium on Bioinformatics Engineering in Industry (SBEI23) — 4 December 2023
- ABACBS Conference — 5-6 December 2023
- Workshops and other satellite events — 7-8 December 2023

All events were held at Hilton Brisbane (190 Elizabeth St, Brisbane City QLD 4000), except workshops which were held at the University of Queensland, St Lucia campus (one workshop, the AHMRN Symposium, was held at the Translational Research Institute, Woolloongabba).
