Alongside these, a Bioconductor Masterclass and the Third Asia-Pacific Bioconductor Meeting also ran on 16 and 17 November respectively.

## Bioconductor Masterclass

**Venue:** SAHMRI Level 4 Boardroom, Adelaide, Australia
**Date:** 16 November 2017

**Overview:** High-throughput sequencing technologies generate large volumes of data that present challenging bioinformatic and statistical problems. This series of hands-on tutorials introduced established and new R/Bioconductor packages and workflows for analysing high-throughput sequence data.

**Course Outline:**

| Time | Session |
|------|---------|
| 9:00am-10:30am | Bioconductor Basics (Martin Morgan) |
| 10:30am-11:00am | Morning Tea |
| 11:00am-12:30pm | RNA-seq analysis with limma, Glimma and edgeR (Charity Law) |
| 12:30pm-1:30pm | Lunch |
| 1:30pm-2:30pm | Gene-set testing for RNA-seq data with EGSEA (Monther Alhamdoosh) |
| 3:00pm-3:30pm | Afternoon Tea |
| 3:30pm-5:00pm | Package Development in R/Bioconductor / Data Visualisation (Martin Morgan) |

The single cell RNA-seq session (scPipe, scater, scran & edgeR, Shian Su) ran 3:00pm-4:30pm.

**Prerequisites:** Intermediate familiarity with R, and basic understanding of biological and technological aspects of high-throughput sequence analysis. Participants were asked to bring a modern wireless-enabled laptop with a web browser installed.

**Intended Audience:** Bioinformaticians and computational biologists interested in using R/Bioconductor for the analysis and comprehension of high-throughput sequence data.

## Third Asia-Pacific Bioconductor Meeting

**Venue:** SAHMRI Level 4 Boardroom, Adelaide, Australia
**Date:** 17 November 2017

**Overview:** The Bioconductor project had undergone significant growth over the preceding 16 years, with over 1,300 packages for high-throughput genomic analysis in the latest release at the time, many developed by researchers from the Asia-Pacific region. This meeting aimed to bring together researchers with an interest in the Bioconductor project to exchange ideas and future plans for software development, consisting of longer talks selected from abstracts, short "lightning" presentations, and software demonstrations.

**Organisers:** Dr Matt Ritchie and Dr Charity Law (Molecular Medicine Division, The Walter and Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research, Melbourne), Mr Stephen Pederson (Bioinformatics Centre, The University of Adelaide).

**Travel scholarships:** A number of scholarships ($500 AUD each) were available for students and Bioconductor package developers to help with the cost of travel to attend the meeting.
