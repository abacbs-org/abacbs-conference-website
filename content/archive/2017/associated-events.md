## COMBINE Student Symposium 2017

COMBINE is the student subcommittee of The Australian Bioinformatics And Computational Biology Society (ABACBS) as well as the official International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB) Regional Student Group (RSG) for Australia. It aims to bring together students from the computational and life sciences for networking, collaboration, and professional development.

The COMBINE Student Symposium ran Monday 13 November, merging into the start of the ABACBS 2017 conference later that evening (see [Program](/archive/2017/program/)).

For more information see [combine.org.au](https://combine.org.au/symposium-2017/).

## Annual General Meeting

The 2017 Annual General Meeting of the Australian Bioinformatics and Computational Biology Society Inc. was held 15 November 2017, 1pm-2pm ACDT, in the SAHMRI Auditorium, Adelaide. Members voted on accepting the minutes and annual report/financial statements of the previous AGM, a number of special resolutions to change the constitution, the membership fee for the next financial year, and election of executive committee members.

Nominations for the executive committee closed 8 November 2017; candidates were given the opportunity to submit a written statement in support of their election, which were circulated to members prior to the election.

## Location

The 2017 ABACBS Conference and COMBINE Symposium were held in Adelaide at the South Australian Health and Medical Research Institute (SAHMRI), located on North Terrace in the CBD of Adelaide, in the centre of South Australia's health and medical precinct.

**Parking:** There is no public parking available at SAHMRI — public parking is available at the Wilson's car park on Hindley Street. SAHMRI guests receive $16 all-day parking (for up to 12 hours), providing they validate their ticket at reception prior to leaving the building. Other parking alternatives are the convention centre and several other lots on Hindley St.

**Smoking:** The SAHMRI building and precinct are smoke free.
