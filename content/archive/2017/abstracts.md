---
title: "Abstract Submission"
---

ABACBS welcomed abstract submissions from students, early-career researchers, professional bioinformaticians and more established researchers, for research topical to bioinformatics and computational biology that was in progress or had been recently published. The presenter had to identify themselves as the corresponding author during submission, and accepted presenters were required to present the work themselves and to register and pay to attend the conference. A presenting author could present only one oral talk at ABACBS 2017.

- **Abstract submission deadline** (including travel bursary application): Friday 13 October 2017, 5pm AEST.
- **Word limit:** 300 words. Title, authors and affiliations of all authors had to be provided (not included in the word limit).
- There were both **long and short-format oral presentations**, with the long format primarily intended for more senior researchers (though also suitable for a student presenting a significant body of work).
- There were **two poster sessions**, plus two **fast forward poster** sessions showcasing selected posters.
- Submitters indicated whether they wished to be considered for a long or short talk, a poster presentation only, or both — presenters not selected for a talk were offered a poster presentation instead.
- Submitters could also indicate if they wished to submit their abstract to the **COMBINE Student Symposium** (Monday 13 November) — a separate registration for COMBINE was required.
- **Prizes** were awarded for the best oral and poster presentations.

## Travel Bursary

Thanks to the conference's generous sponsors, ABACBS was able to provide a number of travel bursaries to support interstate students attending ABACBS 2017. Applicants indicated interest in a bursary as part of their abstract submission; bursaries were awarded based on evaluation of submitted abstracts by the organising committee. Approximately 20 bursaries to the value of $250 each were awarded. To be eligible, applicants had to be a current financial member of the society, have registered for the conference, and have submitted an abstract.

## F1000Research

Presenters were invited to deposit their posters and slides from ABACBS 2017 (for free) to the ABACBS collection on F1000Research, to extend the visibility of their work to a wider audience.
