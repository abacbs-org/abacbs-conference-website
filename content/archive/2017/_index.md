Welcome to ABACBS 2017. The 2017 Australian Bioinformatics & Computational Biology Society (ABACBS) annual conference and the COMBINE Student Symposium were held at the South Australian Health & Medical Research Institute (SAHMRI) in Adelaide, 13-15 November 2017. The meeting showcased the cutting edge of research in the field through a program of invited international and national speakers, and selected oral and poster presentations.

The COMBINE Student Symposium ran on Monday 13 November, followed by the ABACBS conference on Tuesday 14 and Wednesday 15 November. The ABACBS conference was followed on Thursday 16 and Friday 17 November by several optional satellite workshops and events, including a Bioconductor masterclass, the Third Asia-Pacific Bioconductor Meeting, a workshop on Using Open Science in Bioinformatics Training, and a Train-the-Trainer workshop.

Planned sessions included Clinical Genomics, Bioinformatics Software and Methods, Systems and Network Biology, Cancer, Infection & Immunity, and Plant and Animal Genomics. The 2016 annual conference, held in Brisbane, attracted more than 280 participants.

The conference was convened by A/Prof. David Lynn (EMBL Australia Group Leader, SAHMRI/Flinders), with the 2017 conference organising committee — see [Program Committee](/archive/2017/committee/) for the full list.
