Standard registration closed Friday 27 October 2017, 5pm AEST — due to catering requirements, registration was not possible after this date. There is a limit of 128 tickets for the conference dinner, and the organisers reserved the right to close registration early if the venue reached maximum occupancy.

Information about paying membership fees or joining ABACBS was available on the society's membership page.
