There were two ABACBS poster sessions, each held in the SAHMRI ground-level atrium (right outside the auditorium):

- **Poster Session 1:** Tuesday 14 November, 12:00pm-2:00pm
- **Poster Session 2:** Wednesday 15 November, 12:00pm-2:00pm

Posters had to be printed in **A0 portrait format** (841mm width x 1189mm height) — posters printed outside these dimensions could not be presented. Posters were hung at an assigned position based on poster board number (not abstract number) and had to be hung in the morning of the presenting day and taken down the same evening. Prizes were awarded for the best posters.

## Fast Forward Sessions

Ten posters from each session (20 in total) were selected for a 90-second "fast forward" oral presentation immediately prior to the poster session, in the SAHMRI Auditorium: one slide only, pre-loaded on the morning of the session, no questions taken. Prizes were awarded. See [Program](/archive/2017/program/) for the list of Fast Forward presenters.

## F1000Research

Presenters were invited to deposit their posters and slides from ABACBS 2017 (for free) to the ABACBS collection on F1000Research, to extend the visibility of their work to a wider audience.
