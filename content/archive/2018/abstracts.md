---
title: "Abstract Submission"
---

Abstracts and travel bursary applications closed 17 September (9am). Abstract submission for the Clinical Bioinformatics Symposium closed 28 September. Talk and poster presenters were notified 22 October.

A call for late-breaking poster abstracts ran 24 October – 7 November, with presenters notified 3 and 11 November.

Abstracts were submitted via the [ABACBS abstract submission system](http://abstracts.abacbs.org/).
