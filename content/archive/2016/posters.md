## For all poster presenters (COMBINE16/AB3ACBS)

- All posters must be printed in **A0 portrait** format.
- The poster venue was open daily 8am-5pm, Monday 31 October - Wednesday 2 November.
- Posters could be hung at any time during Symposium/Conference hours.

## The Cube / Fast Forward session

Some posters required an electronic version for display in The Cube during the poster session at 5pm on Tuesday 1 November 2016 — Fast Forward presenters, plus a selection of additional posters (presenters were notified individually if selected).

- Electronic poster format: 1080 pixels wide by 1920 pixels high.
- Electronic posters were due by Wednesday evening, 26 October 2016.
- Fast Forward talks ran 5pm, 1 November, at The Cube: a strict 2 minutes talking time, no additional slides (props encouraged).

## Submission to F1000Research

COMBINE16 and AB3ACBS invited all delegates to deposit their posters and slides, for free, to the COMBINE/AB3ACBS F1000Research channel. Depositing a presentation makes it visible to a wider audience, and allows other researchers to make contact even if they couldn't attend in person. Submissions were preferred by 30 October 2016, though later deposits were still accepted.
