title: "Abstract Submission"
---

Abstract submission for AB3ACBS 2016 is now closed.

Abstracts were submitted through an online form covering both the COMBINE Symposium (31 October) and the AB3ACBS Conference (1-2 November), with fields for:

- Full title (truncated at 250 characters)
- Authors and affiliations
- Abstract text (truncated at 2000 characters, approx. 300 words)
- A short note on why the presentation would be engaging to the audience
- Presentation format preference: oral only, poster only, or oral or poster
- Presentation forum preference: COMBINE Symposium only, AB3ACBS Conference only, or both
- An opt-in to have the poster/presentation uploaded to [F1000Research](http://f1000research.com)
- An opt-in to give a 2-minute Fast Forward presentation of the poster
- An optional Student Travel Bursary application (maximum $500 requested from ABACBS, subject to available funding)

The exact submission deadline was not stated on the surviving form or pages.
