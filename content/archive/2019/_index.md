The International Conference on Genome Informatics (formerly known as Genome Informatics Workshop or GIW) is the longest running international bioinformatics conference. It is run by the Association of Asian Societies for Bioinformatics.

The ABACBS Annual Conference is the national bioinformatics conference in Australia and now in its fifth year.

This year these two conferences joined forces to bring together members from all over Australia, the Asia-Pacific region and the world to enjoy the opportunity to interact and hear fantastic stories about genomics, bioinformatics and computational biology research with an emphasis on how advances in computational and statistical techniques are applied to solve important biological and biomedical problems.

The joint conference featured international and national keynote speakers, oral presentations selected from full-length paper submissions and abstracts, and posters selected from abstracts.

## Key Themes

- Genomics and integrative analytics
- Precision medicine
- Single cell analytics
- Non-human, agricultural, environmental and microbial genomics
- Proteomics and metabolomics
- Methods development and reproducibility research

## Overview

The conference was convened by Prof. Tony Papenfuss (Conference Chair & Association of Asian Societies for Bioinformatics Representative), Prof. Shoba Ranganathan and Prof. Jinyan Li (GIW Conference Co-convenors), and Prof. Jean Yang and A/Prof. Mark Cowley (ABACBS Conference Co-convenors), and ran 9-11 December 2019 at the Charles Perkins Centre and Wallace Theatre, University of Sydney, plus associated workshops and symposia on 9 and 12 December.

The conference featured:

- 8 international keynote speakers
- 4 national keynote speakers
- Oral presentations selected from full-length paper submissions (GIW Papers) and abstracts (ABACBS Talks)
- Fast Forward talks and poster sessions
- COMBINE/AYRCOB Student Symposium (9 December)
- A day of workshops and a Clinical Bioinformatics Symposium (12 December)
