---
title: "Abstract Submission"
---

Full paper submissions closed 28 June 2019 (extended from an initial 14 June 2019 deadline), with initial notification of acceptance 16 August 2019 and final notification 20 September 2019.

Talk & poster abstract submissions closed midnight 8 September 2019 (extended from an initial 1 September 2019 deadline). A call for abstracts for late-breaking posters ran through to 22 November, with late-breaking poster presenters notified 29 November.

Abstracts and full papers were submitted via the [ABACBS abstract submission and travel bursary application system](http://ec2-34-222-93-179.us-west-2.compute.amazonaws.com/).
