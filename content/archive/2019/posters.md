## Fast Forward Talks

Fast Forward presentations were held on 10 December, 3:15pm at the CPC Hub Lecture Theatre.

- The length of each fast forward presentation was 1 minute 30 seconds.
- Each presenter was allowed exactly one slide, with a maximum 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Fast forward talk slides were sent to Ellis Patrick at least 24 hours prior to the event, and aggregated into one final PowerPoint document. Slides in PDF or Google Slides format were converted into PowerPoint format. Animations and videos embedded within the PowerPoint document were allowed.
- Fast Forward presenters also presented their posters at the second Poster Session on 11 December.

There were 12 Fast Forward Talks and 155 Posters Only presentations (167 posters in total), with 163 presenters in total across posters and fast forward talks.

## Poster Sessions

Posters had a maximum size of A0 in the portrait orientation. Poster boards were located in CPC Dry Lab 1.3, next to the parallel session room CPC Dry Lab 1.2. Poster numbers were randomly assigned.

- **First Poster Session** — 10 December, lunch time 12:20 - 2:15pm — Poster Numbers 1-85
- **Second Poster Session** — 11 December, lunch time 12:20 - 1:30pm — Poster Numbers 86-170

Presenters with multiple posters were allocated consecutive poster numbers, each marked with an asterisk, arranged side-by-side in the same row in the same poster session. Posters were mounted in the morning prior to the start of each poster session, and de-mounted immediately at the close of each poster session.

## Social Media

Presenters who did not want their fast forward talk or poster imaged, discussed, or shared on social media could indicate this clearly — either verbally at the start of a fast forward talk, or via a Post-it note or similar next to a poster.
