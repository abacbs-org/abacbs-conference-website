## COMBINE/AYRCOB Student Symposium 2019

COMBINE is the student subcommittee of The Australian Bioinformatics And Computational Biology Society (ABACBS), as well as the official International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB) Regional Student Group (RSG) for Australia. It aims to bring together students from the field of computational and life sciences for networking, collaboration, and professional development.

For 2019, COMBINE joined with the student group of the International Conference on Genome Informatics (formerly known as Genome Informatics Workshop; GIW), the Asian Young Researchers Conference on Computational and Omics Biology (AYRCOB).

The COMBINE/AYRCOB symposium was held on the 9th December, and merged into the start of the GIW/ABACBS-2019 conference later that day (see [Program](/archive/2019/program/), Day 1, Session 1).

For more information see [combine.org.au](https://combine.org.au/).

## Annual General Meeting

The 2019 Annual General Meeting of the Australian Bioinformatics and Computational Biology Society Inc. was held 10th December 2019, 1:15pm-2:15pm AEDT, at the Charles Perkins Centre, University of Sydney.

## Location and Hotels

The Joint GIW/ABACBS 2019 Conference was held at the University of Sydney, at the heart of Sydney — one of the most vibrant metropolitan cities of Australia. The University of Sydney is conveniently located just 10 minutes away from the central business district of Sydney. There are many hotels around the University, with easy access to restaurants, local transportation and tourist attractions.

The conference venue was the University of Sydney, with the Plenary sessions held at the Wallace Theatre and the Parallel and Poster sessions held at the Charles Perkins Centre. The two locations are around 3 mins walking distance apart.
