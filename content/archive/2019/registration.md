The organizing committee has worked to keep the registration fee for the joint GIW/ABACBS conference as low as possible. We thank the many generous sponsors for helping to make this possible. The registration fee depends on (1) whether you are a student or not, (2) whether you are a current ABACBS member or not, and (3) whether you register by the early bird deadline or not. All fees are shown in Australian dollars.

Early Bird rates were available until Friday 8 November.

Participants could register just for the workshop(s) without registering for the conference.

## Conference Dinner

The conference dinner was held on the 10th December, the second evening of conference. It was held in the Grandstand Function Centre opposite the conference location, the Charles Perkins Centre. It was a two-course dinner with a dessert buffet. The cost was $65 per person. The conference dinner was sponsored by the Garvan Institute of Medical Research, Kinghorn Centre for Clinical Genomics.

## Online Streaming via Zoom Platform

The committee decided that, for 2019, the full conference would be live-streamed for registered participants via Zoom, as an additional option for any members of the GIW/ABACBS community that would otherwise be unable to participate in person. Participants taking this option were still required to register at the full conference rate.

## Childcare

A pop-up childcare was offered for children under 5 years old whose parents were attending GIW/ABACBS-2019.

## Associated Symposium and Workshops

A few workshops and a Clinical Bioinformatics Symposium ran on the day after the conference (12 December, Thursday). See the [Workshops](/archive/2019/workshops/) page for details.
